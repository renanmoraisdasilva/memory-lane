Deliberately not in v0.1

Advanced search and saved presets · favorites, collections, tags, and ratings · RAW develop, crop, or rotate · GPS maps · CSV/JSON audit exports · delete/cleanup/keep-only actions · drive unmount, SMART, and IOPS diagnostics · NAS or internal folder sources · cache relocation · heuristic date inference · validation history panels · telemetry settings · multi-drive combined browsing · partial backups.
verified_user

Read-only by design: every screen uses catalog-only language. Actions that would touch the real filesystem show a prototype toast instead. The production app has no IPC channel for deleting, moving, renaming, or editing original files.
